# The Sky-Blind Spire

An adventure location by Michael Prescott, run with
[Tunnel Goons](help:tunnel-goons). One session, three to five players.

Nothing on this page is a secret. It is what your character could learn in a
tavern, plus the rulings the table has agreed on. The room key is the
referee's.

## What you have heard

A needle of pale stone rises from the crags above a great lake, its top pierced
by iron rods. The kastromancer Titardinal built it three hundred years ago and
fell to his death before he finished whatever he was doing. Every room inside
is engraved with a number, cut deep into the floor.

People go up for the silver. Not many come down.

Goblins are camped below the crag. Something in the tower has been eating the
lake's quiet.

## What you will find out in the first ten minutes

Entering the first room gives you a tiny orbiting spark of cold light. Walk the
rooms in numerical order and it grows. Step into a room out of order and it is
gone, silently, and you start again.

One hour after you go inside, you stop being able to see the colour blue. See
[Spire rulings](help:spire-rulings) for how that is run, because it is the one
thing worth understanding before you walk in.

## Your character

Seven pregenerated goons are in the module window, plus a blank sheet if you
would rather roll your own. Every one starts with HP 10 and Inventory Score 8,
and two points spread across Brute, Skulker, and Erudite. That is what a
starting Tunnel Goons character is.

Each pregen carries a hook that points at the top of the tower. Your referee
will ask what you would wish for. Have an answer.

## Your inventory is cards

Items are dealt to you as cards, and the cards are your inventory. Each one
says what it gives you in the language of the roll, and how many slots it eats.
Carry more slots than your Inventory Score and every Brute and Skulker roll
takes -1.

A sack of silver is four slots and needs two hands. Greed is the real trap in
this tower.
