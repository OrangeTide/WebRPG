# Tunnel Goons

Rules-light dungeon crawling by Nate Treme, Highland Paranormal Society.
Version 1.2. The whole system fits on a page, and this is that page.

Get the rulebook from the author:
[natetreme.itch.io/tunnelgoons](https://natetreme.itch.io/tunnelgoons). The
SRD is at [tunnelgoons.com/srd](https://tunnelgoons.com/srd).

## The only roll

**2d6 + ability + 1 per helpful item.** Meet or beat the Difficulty Score the
referee names. That is the whole system.

| DS | Difficulty | Feels like |
|----|------------|------------|
| 8  | Easy       | You would probably manage this on a bad day. |
| 10 | Moderate   | Skill or the right tool decides it. |
| 12 | Hard       | Something is genuinely against you. |

The Roll Check panel on your character sheet does this for you. Pick the
ability, tick the items you are actually using, and enter the DS if you know
it. You usually will not, so leave it blank and let the referee compare.

## Danger

If the action could hurt you, the **difference** between your total and the DS
is damage.

- You meet or beat it: the other side takes the difference.
- You miss it: you take the difference.

A monster's DS is also its HP, and monsters never roll. Beat a DS 10 creature
with a 13 and it takes 3, leaving it on 7.

Armour reduces damage you take, point for point.

## Abilities

- **Brute** — smacking things, feats of strength, resisting poison.
- **Skulker** — sneaking, aiming, balancing, dodging.
- **Erudite** — reading, perception, speaking, remembering.

Magic is Erudite plus the right item. There is no spell list. Say what you are
doing and what you are doing it with.

## Items

One relevant item is +1. Two genuinely different items that both apply can be
+2, if you can name what each one does.

Carry more items than your **Inventory Score** and every Brute and Skulker roll
takes -1. Loot is heavy. That is the point. The inventory window counts slots
for you and says when you are over.

## The map is an aid, not a rulebook

Tunnel Goons has no skirmish system: no squares, no move rates, no facing, no
attacks of opportunity. Keep movement freeform. The map window is there so
everyone can point at the same thing and agree where they are, and the software
does not enforce any of it. Drag a token where the fiction says it went.

## Inventory cards

Cards with slot pips are borrowed from Knave rather than being part of Tunnel
Goons 1.2, and they are a common thing to borrow. The rule they make visible is
already in Tunnel Goons: carry more than your Inventory Score and every Brute
and Skulker roll takes -1.

Deal cards from the module window, and the inventory window totals your slots
and tells you when you are over. The optional condition cards (fatigue, stress,
scars) take a slot each and are Knave-style too; ignore them if you would rather
not track that.

## Damage, death, healing

Damage comes off HP. At **0 HP you are dead**. You get lost HP back by spending
the night somewhere safe, and there is nowhere safe inside a dungeon.

## Making a goon

Name your character. **HP 10. Inventory Score 8.** Roll d6 on each of the three
tables in the module window: Childhood, Profession, and During the War. The
first two each give +1 to an ability and an item, the third gives an item. Then
take 2 rations, a cloak of your chosen colour, and one item of your choice.

A starting character has two ability points in total. That is correct.

## Between sessions

Level up every two sessions. Each level: raise one ability by 1, and raise
either HP or Inventory Score by 1.
